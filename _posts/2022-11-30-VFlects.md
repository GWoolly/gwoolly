As VFlects: Battle for Virtua Prime moved from prototype to development, I pivoted from being QA Lead to Level Design for this NFT blockchain game. Dissatisfied with traditional paper-prototyping methods, I created a custom deck-shuffler in batch script and utilized the Tiled level editor software to simulate the game’s virtual tabletop in order to streamline testing and give a more realistic feel for the final product. I collaborated with the design team on developing unique card packs for character moves and attacks and crafted multiple levels, both during the paper design phase and via the game's proprietary level editor.
